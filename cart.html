Round 1

A cart drawer that opens over the page instead of a separate cart page.

  • The bag icon opens the cart on every round 2 page. On the product page, Add to cart adds The Crow and opens it.
  • Each item shows its photo, name, color and size, price, quantity and a remove button. The total updates as you change them.
  • Free shipping is confirmed at the top once the cart reaches $200.
  • Discount opens a field for a discount code.
  • Checkout sits above Shop Pay, PayPal and Google Pay.
  • An empty cart shows Continue shopping, plus a Log in link for shoppers who aren't logged in.
  • On a phone the drawer fills the screen.
